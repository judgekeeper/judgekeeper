shield logo as the name or logo of your own product.

You are welcome to:
- say that your project uses, works with or is based on judgekeeper;
- show the logo when you write or talk about judgekeeper itself.

Please don't:
- call a fork or a changed copy "judgekeeper", or give it the shield logo;

If you fork the project, give your version its own name and logo.

## Other names

judgekeeper reads results from, or works with, tools and models made by others, such as
promptfoo, DeepEval, Inspect AI, MLflow, Langfuse, Claude, Claude Code, OpenAI models,
Codex and Cursor. These names belong to their owners and are used only to say what
judgekeeper works with. judgekeeper is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by
any of them.
