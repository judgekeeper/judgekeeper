Start in
your project.

You need eval results graded by an LLM-as-a-judge, from promptfoo, DeepEval, Inspect AI, MLflow or your own file.

your project folder

With pip

pip install judgekeeper judgekeeper

With uv

uvx judgekeeper

Needs Python 3.11 or newer, on macOS, Linux or Windows.

The 5 steps.

  1. 1

    Find your results

    Run judgekeeper in your project folder. It finds your eval results and opens the marking page.

    judgekeeper

    Nothing found? Run your eval once, or point to the file:

    judgekeeper path/to/results.json
  2. 2

    Mark your answers Pass or Fail

    Mark each answer the way you would grade it. Your LLM-as-a-judge's grade stays hidden. Every mark is saved, so you can stop and come back.

    When See your result appears, click it.

  3. 3

    See your result

    The result page says if your LLM-as-a-judge is good enough to use, needs work, or if it's too early to tell. It also shows where you disagreed.

    Too early to tell? Mark more answers:

    judgekeeper mark
  4. 4

    Write a better rule and test it

    LLM-as-a-judge needs work? Run:

    judgekeeper fix

    It saves every mistake your LLM-as-a-judge made in one file. Use it to write a better rule, or hand it to a coding agent to help you write one.

    Write your new rule in .judgekeeper/fix/rule.txt and run judgekeeper fix again. It tests the rule and tells you if it's better, worse, or can't tell yet.

    Testing works with promptfoo, DeepEval, Inspect AI and MLflow.

  5. 5

    Re-check after you change your LLM-as-a-judge

    Changed the model, the rule or your eval tool? Run your eval, then:

    judgekeeper check

    It tells you if your LLM-as-a-judge still agrees with you, on the same marks.

Keys and costs

Steps 1 to 3 make no AI calls and need no key. Testing a rule, and some re-checks, use your own key. judgekeeper asks before any AI call and never saves your key.

Your own format

No eval tool? Use a CSV or JSONL file with three columns: input (the question), output (the answer) and verdict (pass or fail).

Your LLM-as-a-judge gives a score? Say where Pass starts:

judgekeeper --pass-if 'score>=0.5'

Your LLM-as-a-judge runs in your own code? Add one line after each grade:

import judgekeeper
judgekeeper.record(input=question, output=answer, score=0.82, pass_mark=0.5,
                   reason=reason, judge="claude-opus-5", rule=criteria, name="Safe wording")

With your own file, judgekeeper can't test a new rule: change it in your eval, run it, then judgekeeper check.

Handy commands

terminal
$ judgekeeper --stop         # close the marking page
$ judgekeeper --new          # start over (old marks are kept)
$ judgekeeper --no-browser   # get the link, no browser
$ judgekeeper --help         # see every option
