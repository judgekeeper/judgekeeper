Privacy

What this website and judgekeeper do with your data. Short version: nothing leaves your computer unless you say so.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts.
  • Fonts and every other file are served from this website. Nothing is loaded from Google or any other company.
  • The browser check reads your file inside your browser. The file is never uploaded, and the page works with Wi-Fi turned off.
  • The company that hosts this site (Railway) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, time and page, to run and protect the service. We don't use them for anything else.

judgekeeper on your computer

  • Your answers, marks and results stay in the .judgekeeper folder in your project.
  • judgekeeper sends nothing to us. There is no server of ours.
  • An AI call happens only when you say yes, and it goes through your own eval tool, with your own key, to your own judge.
  • It never prints your keys or writes them to a file.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. For a security problem, use private reporting, as SECURITY.md explains.
