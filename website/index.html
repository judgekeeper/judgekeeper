Check your LLM‑as‑a‑judge.

See how often it agrees with human labels, in one command.

An LLM-as-a-judge is an AI model that grades the answers of another AI model. Teams use one because reading every answer by hand is slow.

Get started How it works

Free and open source. Runs on your computer, inside your project. No account, no server.

How it works

Three parts. judgekeeper is the third: it checks the judge against a person.

Your app answers

Someone asks your AI app a question. It writes an answer.

An AI judge grades it

A second AI model says pass or fail. It is fast and cheap, and sometimes wrong.

You check the judge

You mark some of the same answers yourself. judgekeeper shows how often the judge agrees with you.

The whole process, in six steps

The Guide walks you through each step. Labeling takes the longest: most people need 10 to 20 minutes.

  1. 1InstallInto your project, like pytest.
  2. 2ConnectMost eval tools need nothing. Own format? One setup command.
  3. 3StartOne command finds your judge's results.
  4. 4LabelCorrect or Wrong, in your browser.
  5. 5ResultHow often your judge agrees with you.
  6. 6ImproveSee its mistakes, ask it again, try a new judge.

Install it in your project

judgekeeper is a developer tool, like pytest. It goes into your project's own Python environment, never onto the whole computer.

You need Python 3.11 or newer. No Python? Get it from python.org, then come back.

Terminal

cd your-project
source .venv/bin/activate
pip install judgekeeper
judgekeeper --version

PowerShell

cd your-project
.venv\Scripts\activate
pip install judgekeeper
judgekeeper --version

What you will see

✓ judgekeeper 0.2.0 is ready
Next: run judgekeeper start

Uses uv or Poetry? uv add --dev judgekeeper or poetry add --group dev judgekeeper. No .venv yet, or something went wrong? The Guide shows how.

What you get

Plain sentences first, then the numbers. Every number comes with how sure it is.

  • How often your judge passes the answers you marked Correct, and fails the ones you marked Wrong.
  • Whether it agrees with you often enough to use as a gate, in one line.
  • Where it went wrong, so you can fix its rule.
  • Your labels and results stay on your computer, in your project.
