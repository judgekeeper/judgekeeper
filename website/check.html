Is your judge really judging?

Drop your results file: promptfoo, DeepEval, or your own CSV or JSONL file. This page runs judgekeeper's free check on it: did your judge give a real Pass or Fail to every answer?

  • promptfoo: the file from promptfoo eval -o results.json
  • DeepEval: one run at a time: .deepeval/.latest_run_full.json, or one test_run_….json from your results folder. Older runs are not read here. The .deepeval folder is hidden: on a Mac, press Cmd Shift . in the file picker to see it.
  • Your own file: a CSV, TSV or JSONL file with the columns input, output and verdict (or judge_verdict, judge)

Your file never leaves this page: it is read on your computer. Turn off Wi-Fi and it still works.

What you will see

  • Which judge graded your answers, and its rule
  • How many answers it passed and failed
  • Answers where it made no real decision: a failed call, a reply it could not read
  • Whether it passed (or failed) every single answer

No AI call, no key, no account.

This shows whether your judge is really judging, not whether it agrees with you. To see that, run uvx judgekeeper in your project and mark some answers.

uvx judgekeeper
