judgekeeper

Free and open source, MIT license. It shows how often your judge agrees with you; it never decides for you.

Not affiliated with the tools it can read results from, or their models. Names belong to their owners: see the name and logo note.

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The tool runs on your computer and sends nothing to us.
